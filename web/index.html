About the Little 500

Since 1951, the Little 500 has been the ultimate collegiate cycling event. Teams of four riders compete in a grueling 200-lap race around a quarter-mile cinder track, exchanging riders strategically to maintain speed and avoid fatigue.

Little Six brings this tradition to the web, letting you experience the thrill of team cycling from anywhere.
